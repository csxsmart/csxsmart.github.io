About关于我

I am an undergraduate in Applied Physics at the School of Physics, Southeast University (2023–present). I work on AI for Science: building generative and operator-learning models that predict, reconstruct, and simulate physical systems. 我是东南大学物理学院应用物理学专业的本科生（2023 年至今）， 从事 AI for Science 研究：构建用于预测、重建与模拟物理系统的生成式与算子学习模型。

Spatiotemporal Prediction & Reconstruction of Sparse Physical Fields多通道自适配架构下的稀疏物理场时空预测与重建

Multi-channel adaptive deep-learning architecture · Advisors: Prof. Jianwei You & Prof. Yiqian Mao (School of Information Science and Engineering, SEU)多通道自适配深度学习架构 · 指导教师：游检卫教授、毛艺潜教授（东南大学信息科学与工程学院）

A unified three-stage framework for predicting and reconstructing physical fields from heterogeneous, sparse sensor observations (geophysics, meteorology, aerospace) — where sources differ in channel count and spatiotemporal resolution. 一个统一的三阶段框架，用于从异构、稀疏的传感器观测（地球物理、气象、航空航天）中预测与重建物理场 —— 这些数据源在通道数与时空分辨率上各不相同。

  • Stage 1 — Multi-channel adaptive autoencoder: a shared trunk with channel-wise input/output adapter heads handles any number of channels; zero-init residual ResBlocks (GroupNorm / GELU) and dual-branch down/up-sampling keep deep training stable; a softclip2 latent saturation function suppresses latent blow-up.阶段一 —— 多通道自适配自编码器：共享 Trunk + 通道级输入/输出适配头，使单一编码器可处理任意通道数；零初始化残差 ResBlock（GroupNorm / GELU）与双分支下/上采样保证大深度训练稳定；softclip2 隐空间饱和函数抑制潜变量爆炸。
  • Stage 2 — Latent temporal Transformer: a Transformer encoder with learnable positional encodings over latent sequences, using einops for efficient (B,T,C,H,W) ↔ (B,T,D) reshaping and configurable autoregressive multi-step forecasting.阶段二 —— 隐空间时序 Transformer：在 latent 序列上构建带可学习位置编码的 Transformer Encoder，借助 einops 高效实现 (B,T,C,H,W) ↔ (B,T,D) 重排，并支持可配置的自回归多步预测。
  • Stage 3 — EDM-style diffusion refiner: a Diffusion UNet with sinusoidal time embeddings and multi-scale skips, using a 2nd-order Heun sampler to recover pixel-level detail in just 4–8 steps.阶段三 —— EDM 风格扩散精修器：带正弦时间嵌入与多尺度跳跃连接的 Diffusion UNet，采用二阶 Heun 采样器，仅 4–8 步即可恢复像素级细节。

Independently designed and implemented the multi-channel adaptation modules and the latent diffusion-refinement pipeline; markedly improved reconstruction and multi-step prediction accuracy over baselines. Rated a Top 5% project.独立设计并实现多通道适配模块与隐空间扩散精修流程；重建与多步预测精度较基线显著提升，项目获评 Top 5% 优秀结项。
